# WhatsApp Chat Import Guide

## How to Export Chat from WhatsApp
1. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
2. Open the desired conversation (e.g., Rahul).
3. Tap the **Three Dots (⋮) > More > Export Chat**.
4. Choose **Without Media**.
5. Save the `.txt` file to your computer.

## How to Import in ChatPilot AI
1. Click **Import WhatsApp (.txt)** on the sidebar.
2. Select your exported `.txt` file.
3. ChatPilot will parse all dates, timestamps, and senders.
4. Choose which sender is **YOU** (e.g. Thoufikur Rahman).
5. Confirm the Contact name.
6. Click **Confirm & Import Chat**.
7. All messages, summaries, and outgoing writing samples are automatically populated into the database!
